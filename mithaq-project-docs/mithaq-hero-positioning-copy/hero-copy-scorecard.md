## Recommendation

Recommended direction: **Direction B — Professional Readiness**.

Strategic reasoning:

- It directly answers what Mithaq is: practical legal training.
- It names the main audience without over-narrowing: law graduates and early-career legal professionals.
- It states the practical transformation without unsupported outcomes.
- It keeps the primary CTA conversational and WhatsApp-safe.
- It works in Arabic without sounding decorative or overly literal.
- It protects Scene 03 by leaving deeper “gap” messaging for the next scene.
- It fits the Scene 02 visual comp: editorial hero copy with Seal atmosphere and CTA above the fold.
