# Hero Copy Directions

## Source Constraints

- Mithaq is a premium bilingual 3D legal academy portfolio / landing experience.
- Core concept: The Covenant Seal.
- The gavel is the ceremonial trigger; the Seal is the brand hero.
- Primary conversion: WhatsApp.
- Secondary conversion: simple three-field inquiry / register interest form.
- Audience center: law graduates and early-career legal professionals.
- Support audience: legal-adjacent professionals where level-fit is clear.
- No LMS, dashboard, course marketplace, law firm, booking/payment system, fake urgency, fake proof, or unsupported claims.
- Hero copy must remain understandable without WebGL or motion.

---

## Direction A — Practice Gap

| Component | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| Positioning angle | Focuses on the gap between academic legal study and real legal practice. |
| English headline | Bridge the Gap Between Legal Study and Practice |
| English supporting line | Mithaq helps law graduates and early-career lawyers build the research, writing, and professional judgment that real legal work expects beyond the classroom. |
| Arabic headline | من الدراسة القانونية إلى ممارسة أكثر جاهزية |
| Arabic supporting line | تساعد ميثاق خريجي الحقوق والمحامين في بداية الطريق على بناء مهارات البحث والصياغة والاستعداد المهني التي يتطلبها العمل القانوني الحقيقي. |
| Primary CTA | Ask About Mithaq Workshops / اسأل عن ورش ميثاق |
| Secondary CTA | Discover the Mithaq Method / اكتشف منهج ميثاق |
| Rationale | Strongest problem-recognition direction; it names the study-to-practice gap without shaming universities or learners. |

## Direction Notes

Direction A is problem-led, Direction B is outcome/readiness-led, and Direction C is brand-concept-led. They are intentionally different strategic angles, not minor wording variations.

All directions avoid registration certainty, pricing, dates, seat availability, certificates, accreditation, testimonials, and employment outcomes.
