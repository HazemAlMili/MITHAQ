Mithaq should combine:

## 2. Current Mithaq Decisions

This moodboard respects the current locked direction:

- Mithaq is a premium bilingual 3D legal academy portfolio / landing experience.
- Mithaq is not an LMS, dashboard, booking system, payment system, law firm website, or operational course platform.
- Core concept: The Covenant Seal.
- Opening direction: scroll-driven judicial gavel trigger into Mithaq Seal reveal.
- The gavel is the trigger, not the hero.
- The Mithaq Seal is the recurring brand motif.
- 3D style: symbolic realism.
- Primary conversion: WhatsApp.
- Secondary conversion: simple 3-field inquiry form.
- Delivery approach: Vertical Slice First.
- P1.05 feasibility decision: Option C - Vertical Slice Only Until Asset Optimization.
- Sound effects may exist later, but must be optional, controlled, user-safe, and professional.
- MVP planning is bilingual.
- No fake urgency, countdowns, seat counters, or cohort/deadline pressure systems.
- Placeholder workshop pillars and mentor placeholders may be used for planning only.

## 15. Lighting Direction

Lighting rules:

- Prefer one warm directional source.
- Darkness must feel premium, not scary.
- Shadows should be soft but deep.
- Gold accents should catch light, not glow everywhere.
- Desk texture must remain readable.
- Seal should feel revealed, not magically activated.
- Gavel impact light should be short and restrained.
- Avoid blue sci-fi rim lighting.
- Avoid horror underlighting.

## 16. Composition Direction

Composition rules:

- One hero object should dominate attention.
- The seal should feel centered, ceremonial, and official.
- The gavel should feel present but secondary.
- Negative space must protect copy readability.
- Editorial text must never compete with the 3D object.
- Documents should feel ordered for method/trust scenes, and meaningfully fragmented only for the "gap" scene.
- Mobile compositions must crop without losing the seal/gavel relationship.
- Arabic and English layouts need extra room for text expansion.
- Avoid putting important text inside images or 3D textures.
- CTA environment should be calm, visible, and conversion-ready without pressure mechanics.

## 17. Bilingual / RTL Notes

Bilingual implications:

- Arabic typography needs generous line height and spacing.
- Arabic headings may need different balance than English headings.
- Do not rely on English-only layout references.
- Arabic should not be decoration only.
- The Mithaq Seal may include Arabic, but meaningful content must also exist in DOM later.
- RTL and LTR layouts should be composed independently, not merely mirrored.
- Avoid text baked into imagery because translation, accessibility, and responsive behavior become brittle.
- Bilingual references should inform hierarchy, not final fonts. Typography selection belongs to P2.03.

Sound feeling:

- Deep
- Short
- Restrained
- Wooden / brass resonance

- Professional
- Optional and user-safe

Sound must not be:

- Theatrical
- Explosive
- Horror-like
- Movie-trailer impact
- Loud by default
- Autoplay-dependent

Motion mood:

- Slow
- Decisive
- Scroll-led
- Weighted
- Controlled
- No bouncing
- No playful overshoot
- No spinning seal gimmicks
- No forced long intro trap

Opening implication:

The gavel trigger should feel like a formal signal. The seal reveal should feel earned and ceremonial, not magical or aggressive.

Mithaq should feel like entering a dark, calm legal chamber where a covenant is made visible through a restrained brass seal, while the content remains editorial, bilingual, and conversion-ready.
