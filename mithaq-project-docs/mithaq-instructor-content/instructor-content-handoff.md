# Instructor Content Handoff

## Recommended Profile Order

No public profile order is recommended yet because no confirmed instructor inventory exists.

When profiles are confirmed, order may be based on:

1. Relevance to current confirmed workshops.
2. Breadth of practical training relevance.
3. Balance across research, writing, readiness, and practice areas.
4. Client-approved priority.

Do not order instructors by guessed seniority, assumed prestige, or image quality alone.

## Compact-Card Content Limits

- Name: one or two lines.
- Current role: maximum 8 English words; Arabic should remain similarly compact.
- Organization: show only if approved.
- Authority line: 12-24 English words; Arabic should fit in 2-3 short lines.
- Expertise tags: 2-3 tags preferred; 4 maximum.
- Years of experience: hide unless verified.
- CTA: optional and calm, such as `Ask About the Training Team` / `اسأل عن فريق التدريب`.

## Expanded-Bio Boundaries

Expanded bio may include:

- Current professional focus.
- Relevant practical experience.
- Training relevance.
- Confirmed qualifications or affiliations.
- Linked workshop references, only when confirmed.

Expanded bio must not include:

- Full CV histories.
- Unsupported active-practitioner claims.
- Unverified degrees, memberships, or certifications.
- Invented quotes.
- Firm/client confidentiality-sensitive claims.
- Testimonials or proof points.

## Portrait Status and Risks

Current portrait status: no approved portrait files found.

Portraits require:

- File path.
- Resolution.
- Orientation.
- Background quality review.
- Lighting suitability review.
- Cropping risk review.
- Publication consent status.
- Reshoot recommendation if quality is not premium enough.

Do not use stock people as instructor portraits. Do not approve a low-quality portrait simply because it exists.

## Mobile Wrapping Risks

- Arabic role titles may need more vertical room than English.
- Long organization names should wrap below the role, not inside the role label.
- Expertise tags should remain short and avoid dense legal phrasing.
- If no portraits are approved, use a premium placeholder treatment with clear "profile pending" logic, not a fake image.

## Arabic / RTL Considerations

- Use `dir="rtl"` for Arabic profile cards.
- Use `text-align: start`.
- Use logical spacing such as `margin-inline` and `padding-inline`.
- Keep English firm names direction-safe if retained.
- Do not guess Arabic transliterations.
- Avoid inflated Arabic labels such as `نخبة الخبراء`, `خبير بارز`, or `الأفضل`.

## Workshop-Profile Relationships

- Do not assign instructors to workshops until both workshop inventory and instructor links are confirmed.
- If a workshop relationship is confirmed later, keep it semantic HTML, not baked into the P5.09 dossier GLB.
- Workshop profile links should support inquiry, not imply booking, payment, or enrollment.
