# Copy Consistency Review

## Repeated Terms Found

- `Mithaq` appears across all scenes as the product/brand anchor.
- `practical` appears in the hero, training, and conversion areas because it is the central positioning term.
- `professional readiness` appears in Scene 02 and controlled fallback/closing contexts, but is not repeated as every scene headline.
- `workshop` appears mainly in Scenes 02, 06, 09, and 10 where conversion or discovery requires it.
- `verified` appears in Scene 08 because that scene owns proof and evidence.

## Copy Consolidated

- Scene 02 owns the main positioning promise.
- Scene 03 owns the problem, so later scenes avoid re-explaining the education/practice gap.
- Scene 04 explains teaching method.
- Scene 05 explains capability areas.
- Scene 06 introduces workshop discovery without creating individual cards.
- Scene 07 introduces mentor credibility without invented profiles.
- Scene 08 reserves proof for verified evidence.
- Scene 09 introduces FAQ intent without writing FAQ content.
- Scene 10 converts interest into WhatsApp/form action.

## Unsupported Claims Removed

- No employment outcomes.
- No guaranteed readiness or career success.
- No instructor credentials without approved profiles.
- No testimonials, numbers, logos, affiliations, or certificate claims.
- No dates, prices, seat counts, cohort windows, or availability claims.
- No claims that universities fail to teach practice.
- No "leading experts", "world-class", "revolutionary", or superiority language.

## CTA Terminology Decisions

- Primary conversion remains WhatsApp.
- Scene 02 primary CTA remains `Ask About Mithaq Workshops` from P6.01.
- Scene 10 primary CTA is shortened to `Ask on WhatsApp` for final conversion clarity.
- Secondary conversion remains register-interest/inquiry form.
- Workshop-specific CTAs are generic because confirmed workshop inventory is pending.
- FAQ contact fallback uses WhatsApp without presenting it as urgent or mandatory.

## Scene Overlap Resolved

- Scene 04 does not restate all five training pillars.
- Scene 05 does not explain the full method.
- Scene 06 does not invent workshop names or details.
- Scene 07 does not carry proof metrics or testimonials.
- Scene 08 does not create mentor biographies or detailed trust modules.
- Scene 09 does not write FAQ questions and answers.
- Scene 10 does not introduce new proof or urgency.

## Remaining Risks

- The hero remains recommended for client approval, not final-approved.
- Scenes 06-09 depend on major content inputs that are still missing.
- CTA wording may need adjustment once WhatsApp flow, form destination, and legal/privacy language are confirmed.
- Arabic localization in P6.03 may require structural changes rather than literal translation.
- Mobile copy should be tested in real layouts before final line-break approval.
