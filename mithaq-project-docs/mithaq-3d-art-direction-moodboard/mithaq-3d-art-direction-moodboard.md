## 1. Executive Summary

This document defines Mithaq's 3D art direction moodboard for future modeling, shader planning, opening storyboard, scene sketches, and R3F vertical-slice prototyping.

Total references curated: **20**

Final 3D moodboard decision:

**Option D - Seal-Led Macro Legal Chamber**

Meaning:

- The Mithaq Seal is the hero.
- The gavel is the ceremonial trigger.
- The desk is the legal stage.
- The chamber is the atmospheric container.
- Documents/dossiers represent learning and workshops.
- The style is symbolic realism, optimized for performance.

This ticket does not create final GLB assets, final shaders, R3F implementation, final UI, production textures, or final opening storyboard.

Status is **PASS WITH CONDITIONS** because final production-grade 3D still depends on final brand assets, final seal approval, optimized proxy assets, physical/mobile testing, and vertical-slice validation from P1.05's Option C constraint.

## 2. Current Mithaq Direction

- Mithaq is a premium bilingual 3D legal academy portfolio / landing experience.
- Core concept: The Covenant Seal.
- Opening direction: scroll-driven gavel trigger into Mithaq Seal reveal.
- The gavel is the trigger, not the hero.
- The Mithaq Seal is the hero and recurring motif.
- 3D style: symbolic realism.
- P2.01 visual direction: Option D - Hybrid Direction.
- P2.02 colors remain candidate-safe, not final brand-approved UI.
- P2.03 typography direction: Tajawal 700 is the safe Arabic display default; Lemonada is accent-only pending review.
- P1.05 feasibility decision: Option C - Vertical Slice Only Until Asset Optimization.
- Production-grade 3D complexity is not fully approved yet.
- Mobile and reduced-motion fallbacks remain mandatory.
- Avoid horror, game-like 3D, cheap legal stock cliches, and exaggerated courtroom drama.

## 11. Lighting Direction

| Lighting Element | Direction | Avoid |
| ---------------- | --------- | ----- |
| Key light | Warm source from upper-left, directional, creates authority and object shape. | Overexposed studio lighting or flat daylight. |
| Fill light | Very subtle cool/neutral fill only if material readability needs it. | Blue/purple sci-fi fill. |
| Rim light | Muted gold edge on seal/gavel only when needed for separation. | Glossy gold glow everywhere. |
| Seal highlight | Seal catches light through bevels/relief, not self-glow. | Magical glowing emblem. |
| Desk texture readability | Low grazing light should reveal wood grain and paper edges. | Black crush or noisy texture wall. |
| Particle visibility | Dust/foil particles visible only in key light path, low opacity. | Sparkles, explosions, or magical dust bursts. |

## 13. Mobile / Performance Translation

P1.05 constraint:

**Option C - Vertical Slice Only Until Asset Optimization.**

This moodboard does not approve full 3D complexity on all devices.

| 3D Element | Desktop Direction | Mobile Simplification | Static Fallback |
| ---------- | ----------------- | --------------------- | --------------- |
| Gavel | Dark wood/brass macro trigger with controlled descent. | Simplified geometry, fewer close-up rotations, lower DPR. | Premium still of gavel edge or desk shadow; DOM content remains. |
| Seal | Centered embossed brass/gold hero with bevels. | Lower-poly seal, baked/simple material, no heavy post-processing. | Static seal mark on dark desk/paper composition. |
| Desk | Dark wood/leather/paper stage with subtle texture. | One optimized plane/material; minimal props. | Dark desk image/texture with readable overlay. |
| Documents | Floating/stacking paper/dossier cues. | Reduce count, no physics, simple opacity/translate. | Static dossier cards or paper arrangement. |
| Dossiers | Workshop representation via stamped file language. | Use DOM cards with minimal 3D support. | Fully DOM-based workshop cards. |
| Particles | Sparse dust/foil in key light. | Disable or reduce drastically. | None, or very subtle CSS/static grain. |
| Lighting | Warm key, low fill, muted gold edge. | Simplified light rig, fewer dynamic shadows. | Pre-rendered lighting in static asset. |

Performance rules:

- Keep final mobile GLB and texture budgets below P1.04/P1.05 guidance.
- Avoid heavy transparent particles, dense bevel geometry, and expensive post-processing.
- Prefer baked or simple material detail where it preserves the mood.
- Static fallback must feel designed, not broken.

## 17. Final 3D Moodboard Decision

Selected direction:

**Option D - Seal-Led Macro Legal Chamber**

Why:

- Option A, Gavel-Led Opening World, over-centers the most generic legal object and risks making Mithaq look like stock law imagery.
- Option B, Seal-Led Covenant World, is conceptually strongest but needs desk/chamber grounding to avoid becoming a floating decorative emblem.
- Option C, Desk-Led Legal Chamber World, is grounded but not distinctive enough by itself.
- Option D combines the strongest parts: seal as hero, gavel as trigger, desk as stage, chamber as atmosphere, dossiers as learning metaphor.

Final 3D direction sentence:

Mithaq's 3D world should feel like a dark legal desk inside an implied chamber, where a restrained gavel action reveals a muted brass Covenant Seal through material light, embossing, and scroll-controlled ceremony.
