## 1. Executive Summary

This document defines three core audience personas for Mithaq:

1. Law Graduate / Job-Seeking Fresh Graduate
2. Junior Lawyer / Trainee Lawyer Seeking Practical Skills
3. Career-Changer / Adjacent Professional Entering Legal Practice

Across all three audiences, the strongest conversion drivers are practical clarity, mentor credibility, level fit, bilingual confidence, and a low-friction WhatsApp path.

Mithaq should speak to users who feel the gap between legal knowledge and legal practice. The website must show that Mithaq is premium and serious, but also practical, understandable, and human enough to contact without pressure.

Key implications:

- The hero and early sections must quickly explain the bridge from legal study to professional readiness.
- Workshop previews must show skill value, level, and fit, even while final workshop content remains placeholder-based.
- Mentor/trust sections must build confidence without fake credentials, fake metrics, or unsupported claims.
- WhatsApp must remain the primary conversion path, supported by a short inquiry form.
- Bilingual Arabic/English planning must be treated as core audience experience, not a translation layer.
- Mobile behavior is critical because WhatsApp-first conversion naturally pushes users toward mobile.

No UI design, final copywriting, 3D production, or frontend implementation is authorized by this document.

This persona strategy is based on:

- P0.06 Open Questions Resolution: PASS WITH CONDITIONS.

Important assumption:

These personas are strategic audience models, not statistically validated research segments. They should guide UX and content planning until client interviews, admissions data, or real user research provides stronger evidence.

## 3. Current Mithaq Decisions from P0.06

- Mithaq is a premium bilingual 3D legal academy portfolio / landing experience.
- It is not an LMS, booking system, course dashboard, payment system, or operational platform.
- Primary conversion action: WhatsApp.
- Secondary conversion action: simple inquiry form.
- MVP planning is bilingual.
- Opening direction: scroll-driven gavel trigger to Mithaq Seal reveal.
- 3D style: symbolic realism.
- Delivery approach: Vertical Slice First.
- Workshop content can use temporary placeholders based on Mithaq pillars.
- Mentor section can use premium placeholders for now.
- Trust section must be forward-compatible if proof points are unavailable.
- No fake urgency, countdowns, fake seat counters, or cohort/deadline system.
- Phase 1 page scope: `/`, `/register`, `/workshops/[slug]`.

## 10. Message Hierarchy Recommendation

### Primary Universal Message

Mithaq helps ambitious legal learners move from legal knowledge to professional legal readiness through practical, mentor-led training.

This is a strategic positioning sentence, not final website copy.

### Secondary Supporting Messages

1. Mithaq bridges the gap between legal study and real professional expectations.
2. Training focuses on practical legal research, writing, readiness, and judgment.
3. Mentors and instructors bring credibility that must be shown honestly.
4. Workshops are designed around skill fit and professional growth, not generic course volume.
5. Contact is simple: ask through WhatsApp or send a short inquiry.

### Messages to Avoid

- "Become a lawyer overnight."
- "Guaranteed legal career."
- "Best legal academy."
- "Limited seats" unless confirmed.
- "Certificate that guarantees hiring."
- "Learn law fast."
- "Legal advice" framing.
- "Instant professional readiness."
- "Elite-only" language that discourages beginners.
- Discount-first or course-marketplace language.

## 17. Final Audience Strategy Recommendation

Mithaq should prioritize users who feel a serious gap between legal knowledge and professional legal readiness.

The strongest audience center is the law graduate and junior legal professional. The career-changer is valuable but should be supported through level-fit clarity rather than becoming the dominant tone.

Final strategy:

- Lead with practical legal readiness.
- Make workshop fit and level obvious.
- Build trust through mentors, method, and honest proof.
- Use bilingual Arabic/English presentation as a premium credibility signal.
- Keep WhatsApp as the low-pressure human conversion path.
- Avoid platform, LMS, university brochure, government portal, and generic course-marketplace cues.

Production remains conditional on final brand assets, workshop details, mentor details/photos, bilingual copy, WhatsApp number, and technical environment readiness.
