## Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Why It Matters | Mitigation |
| ---- | -------------- | ---------- |
| Designing without real content | The site may look impressive but fail to explain Mithaq | Do not begin final UI or scene design until scene-level content needs are confirmed |
| Workshop content is missing | The workshops section is a key conversion moment | Request at least 3 confirmed workshops or use an "Upcoming Tracks" model |
| Instructor content is weak | Mentors are one of the strongest trust signals | Request bios, titles, expertise areas, photos, and publication consent |
| Trust claims are unverified | Unsupported claims damage credibility and create legal risk | Use only verified numbers, testimonials, affiliations, and approved claims |
| CTA wording is misleading | "Register Now" may be inaccurate if dates/prices are not confirmed | Use "Register Interest" until program details are approved |
| Arabic copy is treated as translation only | If the audience is Arabic-first, weak Arabic copy will feel less premium | Create Arabic-first or professionally localized copy |
| Site becomes too academic | Mithaq should feel practical and professional, not like a university brochure | Connect every section to real legal practice and readiness |
| Site becomes too much like a law firm | Mithaq is an academy, not a legal services provider | Frame content around training, readiness, workshops, and mentors |
